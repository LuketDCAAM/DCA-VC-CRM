- Current performance uses holding value divided by invested capital; valuation saves verify the written row and await company refresh so ownership edits appear immediately. Explicit position FMV and historical quarterly marks remain independent.

- Deal-first investor matching reuses outreach_batches with kind='deal_match' (scoring in lib/outreach/matching.ts); marking a batch item sent links deal_investors and bumps investor cadence, so history and rhythm stay in one place.
- Share prices (investments.price_per_share, current_valuations.last_round_price_per_share, portco_funding_rounds.price_per_share) stay in cents but must keep 4 decimal places of dollars, so always convert them with src/lib/portfolio/pricePerShare.ts helpers instead of the whole-cent money rounding, which would drop the 3rd and 4th decimals.
